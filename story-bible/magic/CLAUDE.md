# CLAUDE.md — story-bible/magic/

**Mostly blank by design.** This is where this world's magic gets decided, using the two guidelines in `../../writing-guide/magic-system/` as tools.

- `world-form.md` — the Magic Management form for the world as a whole, plus how the Seven Domains map onto it. Fill it first.
- `creature-forms/` — one form per kind of being, filled after the world form. `_template.md` is the blank.

## How a field gets filled
1. Write the proposed answer **and the reasoning beside it**: which lore it rests on, which guideline idea it uses, what it would force or forbid later.
2. Mark it `proposed`. It is not canon.
3. The lead takes it to the author. Only his confirmation changes it to `confirmed`.
4. If a guideline choice would force or contradict a plot point elsewhere in `story-bible/`, flag the tension instead of resolving it.

The **"Already in canon"** lists in each form record facts the lore has already fixed. They constrain the form; the form must not contradict them.

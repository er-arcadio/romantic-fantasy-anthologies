# Magic Management: World Form

The world-level form, built from `../../writing-guide/magic-system/magic-management.md`. **Every answer below is open** until proposed with reasoning and confirmed by the author (ticket T-006).

## Already in canon (the form must fit these)
- Magic arrives with divinity: the fairies emerge from the **five elements**; the elves are born of **light** and carry a special magic.
- **Witch magic** comes from the leader of the fallen angels, passed to the first witch; the second angel woman opened an avenue for more witches.
- Witches have a **forbidden magic** that stops aging. Hags never use it.
- The death god gained his power by **consuming souls** (the leader's, which held nothing, then the other angel man's). Power, at least his, can be taken.
- The death god can **create beings** (demons, the first vampire) and **curse minds** (the hags). The first witch could **contain** him.
- Fairies in human form have **no magic**. A fairy's full form is her most powerful.
- Some knowledge is **secret** (the portal; the hags' curse).

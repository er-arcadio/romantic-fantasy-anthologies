# Foreshadowing Map

Seeds and payoffs, checked against the **telling order** (`timeline.md`). Seeded from the world plan at migration; the story-bible keeper extends it as stories are written. Each entry: the seed, where it can be planted, where it pays off.

| Thread | Seed | Pays off |
|---|---|---|
| The evil witch's turn | Era 2: the death god's influence on her; her lost faith when the demons miss | Era 2: jealousy tips her into open evil. Must surprise, but be earned. |
| The death god's portal | Era 3 (told first): unexplained visits, people who know him too well | Era 2 (told second): the evil witch opens it. The first witch never learns of it. |
| The death god's shame about the demons | Era 2: the demons were the first witch's idea | Era 5: he stays silent through the massacre |
| Fairy vulnerability in human form | Era 1: the human form has no magic | Era 3 onward: vampires can kill a fairy only in human form |
| The hags' hunger | Era 6: fairies vanishing without explanation | Era 6: the curse revealed (to the reader; whether characters learn it is open). Era 7: the witches defeat the hags. |
| The elves' isolation | Era 6: shame after the Civil War | Era 7: the open question of whether it fulfils the thesis |

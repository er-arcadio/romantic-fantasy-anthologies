## Cut (do not add without asking)
Succubus (dropped from the world), jinn, Mami Wata, yakshi, lamia, minotaur, golem, incubus, male fae, male dark elf, dhampir, warlock, gargoyle, nymph and dryad, La Llorona, soucouyant, yuki-onna.

## Distinctions to preserve
- **Demon, fallen angel, death god:** the demon is made, and made to seduce; the fallen angel is good that fell and longs for what it lost; the death god is certain he is neutral.
- **Core fantasy shared by the men:** dangerous to everyone else, changed for her. What each changes *from* is in `../writing-guide/gaze-guide.md`.
- **Light elf, dark elf, drow:** the first two are the same people split by belief; the drow are a separate, half-demon people.

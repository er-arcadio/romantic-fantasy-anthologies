# The World

## Where and when
- **Prehistoric Earth, in the age of Pangea**, before an ice age and before the one before that. Everything happens on Earth; there is no other planet.
- Some eras span only a few hundred years. **"Modern" in any era means the present of that era's own timeline**, not today.
- Humans already live on Earth. Among them, at first, are godlike beings who seem to have been brought by divinity.

## The powers above and below
- **God, ruler of the heavens**, supervises human progress, with the angels. God is never shown endorsing or condemning a faction in narration (`../writing-guide/story-laws.md` → 4).
- **The angels** are men and women. Two generations of women angels give up heaven to help humans: first as **fairies**, then as **elves**. Their departure leaves many male angels frustrated and bored, and some are cast out over time.
- **The underworld** is where the first witch contains the death god. Where it is (perhaps another dimension) is open. He cannot leave except through the evil witch's secret portal.

## The elements
Five: **earth (forest), water, air (wind), fire, and soul (spirit)**. Fairies emerge from them, dedicate themselves to one, and conjure new fairies by immersing themselves in it.

## Light
Elves are born of **light** and reproduce by parthenogenesis with **sunlight and love**.

## Where the peoples live by the end
- **Light elves:** the extreme cold (a nod to the elves of Christmas)
- **Dark elves:** beneath the desert sand, in extreme heat
- **Forest fairies:** the woods, and human cities near nature
- **Soul fairies:** among humans everywhere else, blended into society
- **The death god, the demons, and the drow:** the underworld, leaking influence up through the portal
- **Witches:** in the forest guarding fairies, and disguised among humans guarding them

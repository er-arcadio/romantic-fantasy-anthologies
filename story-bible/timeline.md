## Chronology at a glance
1. **Era 1.** Women angels come down as fairies (hints only), then as elves (open help). Elves speed human progress past what God wanted; God lets it stand. The elves split into light and dark.
2. **Era 2.** Four angels are cast out over it. One kills the leader and becomes the death god. The leader makes the first witch; the first witch contains him and has him make the demons. The evil witch turns, opens a portal, and is defeated.
3. **Era 3.** The demons make a demon lord. The death god sends Dracula. Witches are blamed; they ally against demons and vampires. Equilibrium, with romances everywhere, and tension underneath.
4. **Era 4.** The settlement of vampires and humans in the era's own present.
5. **Era 5.** A witch and an elf make the first werewolf. He massacres the demons, kills the demon lord, turns on humans, and is ended by the fairies.
6. **Era 6.** Fairies and elves go to war over the surviving werewolves. Cursed hags secretly feed on fairies. Fire, water, and air fairies go extinct.
7. **Era 7.** Witches guard the remaining fairies and the humans. Secret schools rise. The hags are defeated. An uneasy balance, with the death god quietly winning beneath it.
8. **Epilogue.** Half-births, and the first male elves.

## Proposed thesis angle per era (to confirm)
- **Era 1:** tradition and balance: how much help is too much
- **Era 2:** religious structures: the fallen angels, the murder, divine hierarchy
- **Era 3:** political structures: prejudice against witches, alliances
- **Era 4:** tradition and norms: counter-Western philosophies of living, sex and bonding
- **Era 5:** moral compass: creation gone wrong, the death god's twisted neutrality
- **Era 6:** political structures and moral compass: allies turned enemies over whether some deaths are worth progress
- **Era 7:** tradition and moral compass: witches between protection and seduction

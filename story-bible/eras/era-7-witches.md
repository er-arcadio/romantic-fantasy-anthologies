# Era 7: The Witches' Era

## Protagonists (decided)
**Witches**, with a few side stories.

## The world now
- **Fairies** mostly keep to themselves, and are **sought out by werewolves and fallen angels for love affairs**.
- **Elves** mostly keep to themselves; some are brave enough to help, and **seek out vampires for love affairs**.
- **Fallen angels:** only a few remain, still seeking the elves and the hidden fairies.
- **Witches** work on two fronts: **in the forest**, protecting fairies from the cursed hags; and **disguised among humans**, protecting them from vampires and werewolves while trying not to be seduced.
- **The death god and the demons** move closer to the witches, hoping to pull them away from killing vampires and werewolves.
- **Secret schools of and for witches** rise to counter them. **Would-be witches are recruited only after they turn 18.**
- **Social structures form.** Fairies and elves hide, the death god and demons hide below but leak their influence, and witches blend in.

## The hags' end
Late in the era, **the witches protecting the fairies defeat the cursed hags**.

## How the saga ends (decided)
An **uneasy balance** on the surface, with the death god **quietly winning** beneath it. No side wins outright. Balance is an ongoing practice; his slow influence keeps shaping the world from below.

## The thesis verdict (purpose, community, health)
- **Witches:** all three. Purpose in protecting fairies and humans; community in the covens and schools.
- **Forest fairies:** all three. Purpose in guiding humans near nature; community in each other.
- **Soul fairies:** all three, with fragile health (their human form has no magic).
- **The death god:** all three, uncomfortably on purpose. The strongest purpose of anyone since his origin; a community in the demons and drow he cares for.
- **Vampires: a tragedy of identity.** They want to survive and reproduce; they have compassion but can't help their hunger, and would ask only to be left alone. Some embrace their purpose, some can't stand it.
- **Werewolves: a tragedy.** Community and health, **no purpose**, held back by shame.
- **Fallen angels: a tragedy.** Purpose (wanting their women back), **no community**.
- **Elves: open.** Community and safety in isolation, but their purpose is mostly to stay out of the way.

*Writing note:* the tragedies are deliberate, and are set against the groups who found peace. Those who found peace mostly did it by separating; those still missing something either caused the most harm or couldn't let go.

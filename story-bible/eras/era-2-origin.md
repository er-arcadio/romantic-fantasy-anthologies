# Era 2: The Origin

## Protagonists (decided)
Two main women whose stories collide:
1. **The leader** of the fallen angels.
2. **The first witch**, who inherits the leader's power. She has a human background and is connected to the leader later.

Supporting: **the second angel woman**, who has her own story. **Antagonist:** the evil witch, whose turn should land as a surprise. The death god and the other angel man support; they do not lead.

## Events
1. **The four fallen angels** (two men, two women) are the outcasts of Era 1. Their plan: **end the fairies' and elves' involvement in human progress** and keep the groups separate. The leader runs the movement. They **do not hide**; they want their presence and cause known.
2. One of the men believes they must be **more powerful to truly restore balance**. He convinces the other two to help kill the leader.
3. He **consumes the leader's soul** but doesn't find her power there, because she has already passed it on. He consumes **the other man** right after and gets what he wanted. He becomes the **death god**.
4. **The leader creates the first witch**, passing her power on. **The second angel woman opens an avenue for more witches to be created**, sacrificing herself. **The first witch starts the first coven.** Witches did not exist before this.
5. **Containment.** The first witch **contains the death god in the underworld**, drawing on the power of the two women fallen angels. It costs her a great deal.
6. **The demons.** The first witch realizes she can use the contained death god to make the **demons**: male beings meant to **seduce the progressive elves**. (Male because some good people can be infected by evil, and because men are physically stronger.) **Curse:** a demon's child with a mortal is always male. A **coven sister**, already seduced by the death god, goes to help, secretly meaning to win his heart and join his cause. She becomes **the evil witch**.
7. **The demons miss.** They also seduce the dark elves, many witches, and the fairies. Demons and elves produce the **drow**.
8. **The evil witch's turn**, in three steps: the death god's view of balance through death takes hold of her; she loses faith when the demons miss; and **jealousy** tips her over. The first witch keeps visiting the death god in the underworld, partly to control him and partly from feeling. The evil witch sees it, envies her power a little, and mostly can't bear not being first in his eyes. *Plant the first two steps quietly so the third surprises but makes sense.*
9. **The end of the era.** The evil witch spreads death across the Earth with the death god's support, since he can act only through her. **She opens a secret portal** to let him out. The **coven, helped by the light elves, defeats her**, and **he does not come through to save her**. He learns nothing about his goal, only about method: *"The women before me infected this world little by little over time. Change is slow and unnoticed. I see that now."*

## The portal afterwards
He never uses it for big attacks. He uses it to **visit people**: the near-dead (looking for the right candidate for Dracula); witches and dark elves, to seduce them; and he is accidentally seduced himself, by witches and some fire fairies. Later, female vampires visit him. **No one knows of the portal except those he visits**: some share his cause, others are infatuated and won't risk losing him. **The first witch never finds out.**

## The male fallen angels (later, recurring)
The two generations of women "missionary" angels left many male angels frustrated and bored. After the first four, **more male angels trickle down** over time. They scare the demons off the elves and fairies and want their women back **emotionally** ("that's our women"). Sex comes from desire, instinct, and a feeling of home: sexy at times, tragic at others, because fairies never conceive. Mostly unresolved on purpose.

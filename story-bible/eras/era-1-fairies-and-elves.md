# Era 1: Fairies and Elves on Pangea

**Told in two parts, first generation then second**, braided with Era 4 in the third book.

## Protagonists (decided)
- **Part one:** a **first-generation fairy** who leads the march of the original angels.
- **Part two:** **two elves** who lead the march of the second generation. (Proposed reading, to confirm: they are the two whose beliefs split the elves into light and dark.)

## Part one: the original angels
- A group of **women angels** tell God they will go down as **fairies** to guide humans through their most trying times. They are unified on the mission, **give up their place among the angels**, and are bound by that choice.
- **Fairies only give hints.** They appear as fairies to signal ("come this way"), or blend in as humans without joining society. **In fairy form they never speak to humans**, so as not to tell them which path to take.
- They are always women because a woman's appearance eases nerves in desperate times.
- **Three forms:** small (quick flight, hint-giving, silent); human (can speak, has freckles so the reader knows she is a fairy, **no magic**); full (human height, wings, most powerful).
- **Elements and roles:** fire, water, air, earth (forest), soul. Soul fairies tend humans at birth and death.

## Part two: the second generation
- Hints are not enough. A second group of women angels, inspired by the first, come down as **elves** with integration in mind. They don't hide and they openly help society progress.
- They help so much that they **advance human technology quickly, further from free will than God wanted**. Elves are never called "angels from heaven," though it is easy to read them that way. The founding elves **arrive as adult angels** (decided 2026-10-18). "Born of light" describes their daughters, whom the fairies help raise.
- **God and most angels** judge this the natural course and let it be. **Four angels** disagree loudly, defy God, and are **cast out** (Era 2).
- **The elf split, before the angels fall.** The elves realize they are helping too much, in a biased direction. Two views emerge: **light elves** keep pushing progress (light aesthetic); **dark elves** counter by pulling humans toward temptation and vice (darker aesthetic). Neither is right or wrong, and neither is part demon.

## Rules in force
- Fairies and elves **never cross with other races** in this era.
- No male elves or fairies (`../reproduction.md`).

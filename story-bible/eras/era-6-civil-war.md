# Era 6: The Civil War

## Protagonists (decided)
On opposite sides: **the elf from Era 5**, and **a fairy who helped take down the first werewolf**.

## The sides
- **Fairies** side with humans. They watched humans turned and killed in the massacre; to them the werewolves, and death itself, were wrong. They are angry. **They want the surviving werewolves gone.**
- **Elves** see the massacre as a net good: the demons are gone, and the humans were a small price for a good direction. They believe the werewolves are subconsciously on the humans' side. **They want them kept.**
- **Witches** appreciate both sides' intent but **hate the killing**. They fight for peace and are too weak to match either side.
- **Vampires** don't care, and keep feeding.
- **The death god and the demons** approve (more death) and watch.

## How the war goes
1. **Fire fairies** lead the charge and take the heaviest losses.
2. **Water fairies** are hit hard and become endangered.
3. **Earth (forest) fairies** retreat to the woods before it gets too bad.
4. **Soul fairies** stay out from the start; they are already endangered.
5. Near the end the **elves are winning**, with heavy losses, and the fairies are losing badly.
6. **The drow**, outcast by the elves, join the death god and the demons, traveling between Earth and the underworld.
7. **The cursed hags.** The death god and the drow infect the minds of the few hags on the land with a **hunger that forces them to feed on fairies to survive**.
8. The hags **secretly aid the elves**, consuming many water and air fairies and some soul fairies, and some elves too.
9. **Outcome: fire, water, and air fairies go extinct.** Many forest fairies remain; only a few soul fairies survive.

## Aftermath
- **Elves** lose many. Survivors isolate themselves from humans out of shame and to keep the balance: **light elves in the extreme cold, dark elves beneath the desert sand.**
- **Forest fairies** keep to the woods and to cities near nature. **Soul fairies** blend in among humans everywhere else, protecting them from vampires and werewolves.
- **Werewolves** side with the elves but just want to live among humans in peace and not think about turning. They carry the **shame** of being the root of the deaths.
- A **general balance for the moment**, but vampires and werewolves run rampant and the population falls.

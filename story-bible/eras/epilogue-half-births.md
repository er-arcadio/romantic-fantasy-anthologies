# Epilogue: Half-Births

A coda, not central to the story.

- A wave of **half-births**: half human, half something else. They can be fed on by vampires but cannot become vampires.
- Half-births are how **male elves** enter the world, since a male elf needs a human father.
- Half-born children are **never sexualized**, and violence against them is never framed as erotic (`../../writing-guide/story-laws.md` → 1).

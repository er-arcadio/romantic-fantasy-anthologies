# Era 5: The Great Demon Massacre and the Rise of the Werewolves

## Protagonists (decided)
The **witch** and the **elf** who make the first werewolf.
- The witch rejects forcing equality if it means being evil.
- The elf wants to stand against the **dark elves who bullied her**.
- These motives sit in their subconscious when they desire and design their third, and **surface once they see his potential**.

## Events
1. The witch and the elf want **a man they can share**. In the woods, they turn a **human man** into the first werewolf, in a three-way. Sexy first, then out of control.
2. In the curse's first episode he **kills a demon**, and rumors spread.
3. They **make him stronger** and try to convince him to kill vampires too.
4. His strength outgrows him; **he is not himself in wolf form**. He grows sloppy and bites and infects other human men.
5. The wolves he makes are **more level-headed**: not fully present in wolf form, but acting on instinct and the subconscious, not rage.
6. **The massacre:** the first werewolf kills demons en masse.
7. **Dracula** intervenes, is no match, and retreats with his vampires.
8. The witches hunt for the man in human form and can't find him.
9. He **kills the demon lord**, then turns on humans, killing and converting them. The other werewolves try to stop him but are too afraid.
10. **The fairies unite**, and the fairy leaders of each element strike the final blow together.
11. The surviving demons **retreat to the underworld** with the death god.

## The death god's stance
He is a little **ashamed of the demons** he made with the first witch: built to bring equality, while he still believes only death brings it. So **he doesn't intervene**; the werewolf is serving his cause. He hopes the massacre shows that **there will never be peace while good and evil are locked in a tug of war**.

## Rules
Werewolves never turn women. Their weakness is **beautiful women**.

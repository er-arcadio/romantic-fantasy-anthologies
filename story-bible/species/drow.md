# Drow

**Mission:** ambition and conquest (shared roster entry with dark elves; whether to split it is open).

## Origin
**Half demon, half elf**, born when the demons seduced elves in Era 2. A separate people from the dark elves, though they share some dark aesthetic and the pull toward hedonism. **Distinct purple skin.**

## Sex and lean
Male or female. Women lean elf with slight demon traits; men lean demon with slight elf traits.

## Arc across the eras
Born in Era 2; **outcast by the elves** and joining the death god and the demons in Era 6, traveling between Earth and the underworld; with the death god, they **curse the hags** to feed on fairies.

## Thesis verdict
Part of the death god's community; he gives them a shared purpose.

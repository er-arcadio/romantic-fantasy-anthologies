# Demons

**Mission:** temptation, domination, power. **Appeal type:** the dangerous one.

## Origin
Made in Era 2 by the contained death god, at the first witch's urging, to **seduce the progressive elves**. Male, because some good people can be infected by evil and because men are physically stronger. A coven sister who helped became the evil witch.

## Curse
A demon's child with a **mortal** is always **male**, so the seduction spreads. With elves they produced the **drow**.

## Arc across the eras
Miss their target and seduce dark elves, witches, and fairies (Era 2); unite under a **demon lord** (Era 3); massacred by the first werewolf, who kills the demon lord (Era 5); the survivors retreat to the underworld with the death god (Era 5 onward) and draw closer to the witches in Era 7.

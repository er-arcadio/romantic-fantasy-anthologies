# Fairies

**Mission:** trickery and bargains (archetype); in lore, guiding humans through their most trying times with hints.

## Origin
The first generation of **women angels**, who told God they would go down as fairies and gave up their place among the angels to do it. They are bound by that choice. Always women, because a woman's appearance eases nerves in desperate times.

## Forms (first generation)
1. **Small:** quick flight and hint-giving. Never speaks to humans.
2. **Human:** speaks, integrates, understands. Has **freckles**, so the reader knows she is a fairy. **No magic**, which makes this the one form a vampire can kill her in.
3. **Full:** human height, wings, the most powerful in magic.

Open: whether conjured fairies share all three.

## Elements
Fire, water, air (wind), earth (forest), soul (spirit). **Soul fairies** tend humans at birth and death; they rarely conjure out of shame at immersing themselves in birth or death, so they are endangered early. Some soul fairies drawn to death and birth make exceptions with vampires, and some are seduced by the death god.

## Reproduction and age
Conjured, not born: a fairy immerses herself in her element and conjures an **adult** mental clone with a different look. Fairies **have no age** and **can never conceive** with another being. See `../reproduction.md`.

## Arc across the eras
Hints in Era 1; seduced by demons in Era 2; the fairy leaders of each element end the first werewolf in Era 5; the Civil War against the elves in Era 6, where **fire, water, and air fairies go extinct**; in Era 7, forest fairies keep to the woods and soul fairies live hidden among humans, both sought out by werewolves and fallen angels for love affairs.

## Thesis verdict
Forest and soul fairies find purpose, community, and health (soul fairies' health is fragile).

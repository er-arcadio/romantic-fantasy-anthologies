# Witches

**Mission:** power and knowledge.

## Origin
Did not exist before Era 2. **The leader of the fallen angels created the first witch**, passing her power on; the **second angel woman opened an avenue** for more witches. The first witch founded the first coven.

## Age
Human, and age like humans until they learn the **forbidden magic** that stops aging; then they freeze at an apparent 21 to 27, depending on skill. Those who never use it are **hags**.

## Belief
Share the leader's goal: balance without killing. The **evil witch** is the exception, siding with the death god.

## Arc across the eras
Contain the death god and make the demons (Era 2); face prejudice for the vampires and ally against demons and vampires (Era 3); a witch co-creates the first werewolf (Era 5); fight for peace in the Civil War but are too weak to matter (Era 6); protect fairies in the forest and humans in the cities, found **secret schools** recruiting only after 18, and defeat the cursed hags (Era 7).

## Thesis verdict
All three: purpose, community, health.

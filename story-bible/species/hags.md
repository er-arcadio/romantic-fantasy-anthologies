# Hags

**Mission:** curses and vengeance.

## Origin
A group of witches who **never use the forbidden aging magic**: by conviction, or because they never learn it or learn it poorly. They keep aging and show it. Often work in trios or covens.

## The curse
In Era 6, the death god and the drow **infect the minds of the few hags** with a **hunger that forces them to feed on fairies** to survive. The cursed hags secretly aid the elves, consuming water, air, and soul fairies, and some elves.

## End
Defeated by the witches protecting the fairies, late in Era 7.

# Vampires

**Mission:** hunger and immortality. **Appeal type (men):** the devoted predator.

## Origin
Made by the death god after the evil witch's defeat, as his first slow, infectious move: he sent a dead human's soul back as **Dracula**, the first vampire. Made for **sheer killing**, aimed at witches and elves, though they also kill and recruit humans.

## Strength and limits
Can kill witches, elves, and some demons. **Cannot kill a fairy in full form**, only in her magic-less human form.

## Age
**Vampire law: they turn someone only once that person has reached maturity** (decided 2026-10-18). Since vampires start human, that means 18 human years or older. Those turned look 18 to 30 and stay that age forever.

## Arc across the eras
Arrive in Era 3; settle among humans in Era 4; Dracula retreats from the first werewolf in Era 5; indifferent to the Civil War in Era 6; run rampant with the werewolves afterward; in Era 7, elves seek them for love affairs and witches hunt them.

## Thesis verdict
**A tragedy of identity.** They want to survive and reproduce, have compassion, can't help their hunger, and would ask only to be left alone. Some embrace their purpose; some can't stand it.

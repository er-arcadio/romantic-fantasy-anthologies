# Elves (light elves)

**Mission:** legacy and wisdom (archetype); in lore, openly helping human society progress.

## Origin
The second generation of **women angels**, inspired by the first, who came down as elves with integration in mind. The founding elves arrived as adult angels; their daughters are born of light, and the fairies help raise them. They carry a special kind of magic (open). Never called "angels from heaven," though easy to read that way.

## Belief and look
**Light elves** keep pushing human progress, with a light aesthetic. They split from the **dark elves** over how much help is too much (`dark-elves.md`). Neither side is right or wrong.

## Reproduction and age
Parthenogenesis with **sunlight and love**, usually with another elf. Later with demons (the drow); with human fathers only from the epilogue. Their children use the full aging chart, including the rapid childhood. No male elves until the epilogue's half-births.

## Arc across the eras
Speed human progress past what God wanted (Era 1); help the coven defeat the evil witch (Era 2); begin reproducing with others (Era 3); stay out of the settlement (Era 4); an elf co-creates the first werewolf (Era 5); win the Civil War at heavy cost and cast out the drow (Era 6); live isolated in **the extreme cold**, with some seeking vampires for love affairs (Era 7).

## Thesis verdict
Open: community and safety in isolation, but is staying out of the way a purpose?

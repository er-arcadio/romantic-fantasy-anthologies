# Humans

Living on Pangea from the start, progressing while the gods supervise. Guided by fairy hints, then sped forward by the elves. The majority of every people, human or not, just want peace, love, and sex. Humans are seduced by every kind in the world, turned by vampires and werewolves, and protected in Era 7 by witches and soul fairies.

Humans age normally. A man who becomes a werewolf, or anyone turned vampire, starts human.

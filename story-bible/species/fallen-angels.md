# Fallen Angels

**Mission:** redemption or defiance. **Appeal type (men):** the broken one.

## The first four
Two men and two women, cast out for opposing God's acceptance of the elves' over-help (Era 2). Led by a woman. They do not hide. Their plan: end the fairies' and elves' involvement in human progress. One man kills the leader and becomes the **death god**; the second woman sacrifices herself to open the way for witches.

## The men who came after
The two missions of women angels left many male angels frustrated and bored. **More male angels trickle down** over time, kicked out to live among the rest. They scare the demons off the elves and fairies and want their women back **emotionally**. Mostly unresolved on purpose.

## Thesis verdict
**A tragedy.** Purpose (wanting their women back) but **no community**: few, scattered, still seeking.

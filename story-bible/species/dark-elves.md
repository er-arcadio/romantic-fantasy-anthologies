# Dark Elves

**Mission:** ambition and conquest (archetype); in lore, pulling humans toward temptation and vice to counter the light elves' push.

## Origin
The same people as the light elves, split by belief before the angels fell. Darker aesthetic. **Not part demon** (that is the drow). Neither side is right or wrong.

## Reproduction and age
As elves.

## Arc across the eras
The split (Era 1); seduced by the demons who were meant for the light elves (Era 2); the death god visits to seduce them (Era 2 onward); one group bullied the Era 5 elf protagonist; after the Civil War they live **beneath the desert sand in extreme heat** (Era 6 to 7).

## Open
What exactly the dark elves believe beyond temptation as a counterweight.

# The Death God

**Mission:** balance through death. **Appeal type:** the untouchable one.

## Origin
One of the four fallen angels. He has **always believed in neutrality**. Convinced that only more power could truly restore balance, he had the leader killed, consumed her soul (finding no power, which she had passed on), then consumed the other angel man and got what he wanted.

## Belief
He deludes himself that **killing all magical beings is the only way to true neutrality**. He never learns otherwise; he only changes tactics.

## Confinement and the portal
Contained in the underworld by the first witch. Acts first through the evil witch, then through her **secret portal**, which he uses only for quiet visits: the near-dead, witches and dark elves he seduces, witches and fire fairies who seduce him, and later female vampires. **The first witch never learns of the portal**, though she keeps visiting him below, partly to control him and partly from feeling.

## Arc across the eras
Murder and containment, the demons, the evil witch (Era 2); Dracula (Era 3); ashamed of the demons and silent through the massacre (Era 5); watches the Civil War and curses the hags with the drow (Era 6); draws closer to the witches and **quietly wins** beneath the uneasy balance (Era 7).

## Thesis verdict
All three, uncomfortably on purpose: the strongest purpose of anyone, and a community he cares for in the demons and drow.

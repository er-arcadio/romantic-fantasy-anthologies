# Plot Twist Inventory

Reveals, and the book in the telling order where each lands. A story must not reveal one earlier.

| Reveal | Lands in (telling order) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Witches did not make the vampires | Book 1 (Era 3) | The fairies suspect it first |
| The death god made the vampires (through Dracula) | Book 1 or 2 | Open: how much Book 1 reveals |
| The first witch had the demons made | Book 2 (Era 2) | Recontextualizes the witches' prejudice in Book 1 |
| The evil witch was the death god's lover and ally | Book 2 (Era 2) | Her turn must surprise |
| The death god was once an angel, and the elves the reason he fell | Book 2 and Book 3 | Book 3 (Era 1) shows the origin |
| The cursed hags were secretly helping the elves | Book 5 (Era 6) | Open whether any character learns it |
| The death god is quietly winning | Book 6 (Era 7) | The ending's undertow |

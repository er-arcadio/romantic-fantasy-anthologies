# Ages and Lifespans

**The age rule in `../writing-guide/story-laws.md` → 1 governs everything here.** Maturity (an adult body, mind, and development) decides eligibility for sexual or romantic content.

## Distribution
Apparent ages follow a bell curve with a **standard deviation of 3 years**: women centre on about **24**, men on about **34**. In-world rationale for the men's later centre: **men need more time to develop and to learn to harness their power and skill.**

## How age is counted
- **Humans, witches, vampires, werewolves:** ordinary human years. Maturity at 18.
- **Creatures from god** (fairies, elves, fallen angels, the death god, drow): a **12:1 count**, each human month counted as a year, until their 100th (8 years and 4 human months). After that they call themselves **centennials** when asked their age. Mind and body mature on this clock, so **maturity arrives at a stated 18**. Their age is never given in human years. Beings who arrive or are conjured as adults begin their count at 18.

## The aging chart (apparent age)
Applies to elves, dark elves, drow, and others born rather than made. Human time is shown so the reader can track it; characters never use it.

Women:
| Apparent age | Human time to get there | Their stated age |
|---|---|---|
| Birth to 18 (maturity) | 18 months | 18 |
| 18 to 21 | 15 more years | centennial |
| 21 to 24 | 150 more years | centennial |
| 24 to 27 | 150 more years | centennial |
| 27 | immortals stop here | |
| 27 to 30 (mortals only; "old") | 15 more years | centennial |
| 30 to 33 (final year) | 1 more year | centennial |

Note: a creature reaches centennial (8 years 4 months in human time) while still looking about 19 or 20 on this chart.

Men follow the same chart, except maturity takes **36 months** (stated 36) and arrives at apparent **28**. How the later bands run for men is open (audit question 3, skipped for now).

## Exceptions
- **Humans** age normally.
- **Witches** age like humans until they learn the **forbidden magic** that stops aging, then freeze at an apparent **21 to 27**, depending on skill. (The frozen age reflects what men tend to prefer; a commentary hook.)
- **Hags** never use the forbidden magic, so they keep aging and show it.
- **Vampires** start human and, by their own law, turn someone only after maturity (18 human years or older). They look 18 to 30 and stay that age forever.
- **Fairies** are conjured as adults, ancient minds (ultimately former angels) in clearly adult forms, and begin their 12:1 count at 18.
- **Elves** use the chart in full, including the rapid childhood (18 human months, stated 0 to 17, never in romantic or sexual content).
- **Werewolves** are made from adult humans and keep human years. A werewolf's bite turns an adult; a bitten child dies.
- **Demons:** how they age and count is open (see below).

## Open
- Rapidly maturing children: what they remember, who raises them, how they are treated. (Mind maturing on the 12:1 clock is decided.)
- **Demons:** created as adults by the death god? Do they count 12:1 like creatures from god, or human years? How do demon sons born to mortal mothers age?
- How do slowly aging mortals relate to immortals who don't age?
- The rules of a witch's frozen appearance: cost, limits, who can see through it.
- Whether the death god or other men look different from the bell curve.

# Ages and Lifespans

**The age rule in `../writing-guide/story-laws.md` → 1 governs everything here.** Stated age decides eligibility for sexual or romantic content, never apparent age.

## Distribution
Apparent ages follow a bell curve with a **standard deviation of 3 years**: women centre on about **24**, men on about **34**. Most women look over 21; a smaller group looks 18 to 21; a very small tail is rapidly maturing children.

In-world rationale for the men's later centre: **men need more time to develop and to learn to harness their power and skill.**

## Ages are written as plain stated years
A woman who looks 18 would say she is about 2. The apparent age exists only to give the reader a sense of maturity and physical presence.

## The aging chart
Applies to everyone **except humans, witches, fairies, and vampires** (so elves, dark elves, drow, werewolves, demons, and others).

Women:
| Apparent age | Time it takes | Stated age on reaching it |
|---|---|---|
| Birth to 18 ("maturity") | 18 months | 1.5 |
| 18 to 21 | 15 years | 16.5 |
| 21 to 24 | 150 years | 166.5 |
| 24 to 27 | 150 years | 316.5 |
| 27 | immortals stop here | |
| 27 to 30 (mortals only; "old") | 15 years | 331.5 |
| 30 to 33 (final year) | 1 year | 332.5 |

Men follow the same chart, except maturity takes **36 months** and arrives at apparent age **28**. How the later bands run for men is open.

**Consequence of the age rule:** on this chart, anyone in the apparent 18 to 21 band is stated 1.5 to 16.5 and is never in sexual or romantic content. A woman becomes eligible at stated 18, an apparent age just over 21.

## Exceptions
- **Humans** age normally.
- **Witches** age like humans until they learn the **forbidden magic** that stops aging, then freeze at an apparent **21 to 27**, depending on skill. (The frozen age reflects what men tend to prefer; a commentary hook.)
- **Hags** never use the forbidden magic, so they keep aging and show it.
- **Vampires** start human and, by their own law, turn someone only after maturity (18 human years or older). They look 18 to 30 and stay that age forever.
- **Fairies** are conjured as adults and **have no age**; each is an ancient mind (ultimately a former angel) in a clearly adult form.
- **Elves** use the chart in full, including the rapid childhood.

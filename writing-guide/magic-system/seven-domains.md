# Seven Domains of Magic



## Ranked overview

Reality outranks the other six domains, Time and Space follow, and Life ranks last; each domain carries one color, set by what it feels like to use.

| Rank | Domain | Dominance | Color | Hex | Governs |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Reality | 100% | White (prismatic) | #FFFFFF | What is true, and what could be |
| 2 | Time | 87% | Amber | #E8912D | Sequence, duration, rate of change |
| 3 | Space | 70% | Deep blue | #2F5DA8 | Position, distance, geometry |
| 4 | Matter | 57% | Stone brown | #7A5C3E | What things are made of and how it is arranged |
| 5 | Mind | 53% | Violet | #7B4FBF | Thought, perception, information |
| 6 | Energy | 50% | Red | #D93A2B | Force and radiation |
| 7 | Life | 47% | Green | #3E9B4F | Living bodies and their processes |

Dominance is relative strength: 0% is a person with no magic and 100% is Reality at its ceiling. Each domain is scored 0 to 10 on override, reach, and counterability, then divided by Reality's 30. These are judgment scores, not measurements.


## Why this order

The ranking compares each domain at its BROKEN-tier ceiling, with equal skill on both sides, and scores three things: whether its ceiling can override the other domains, how much of existence it can touch, and how easily an opponent can block it.

1. **Reality.** Its ceiling rewrites the rules the other six run on. Only another Reality user can contest it.
2. **Time.** It can undo any outcome in any other domain by rewinding or skipping it. It ranks below Reality because it changes when things happen, not what is true.
3. **Space.** Every physical power has to reach its target through space, so Space can deny that reach: teleport away, fold distance, or relocate the target. It cannot touch the past, which is why Time outranks it.
4. **Matter.** Structural control can take apart the bodies, weapons, and generators that Energy and Life users depend on. Matter and Energy are near-peers because mass is stored energy; Matter edges ahead because subatomic control can release that stored energy, while Energy control alone cannot rearrange structure.
5. **Mind.** Total cognitive control is decisive against anyone with a mind. In a world of magicians that means controlling the wielder, and with them every domain they command. It does nothing to machines or constructs, and it can be blocked by shielding.
6. **Energy.** It has the strongest ranged output, but it is transient: it dissipates, can be redirected, and acts on matter only through force.
7. **Life.** Its reach is the narrowest: living bodies only. It can harm or revive others in ways that shields built against matter and energy do not stop, but it touches nothing that is not alive. It ranks last on offense and highest on endurance.

This is a ceiling ranking, not a matchup table. A Mind user who lands first can beat a higher-ranked opponent, and a skilled Life user can outlast a careless Energy user.


## Sub-powers by domain

A sub-power is a verb applied to a domain. Scope (self, other, area) and tier (C to BROKEN) then set how strong it is.

The columns run in the order a student learns them, which is also the order they get harder to do well. Sensing comes first. Destroying comes next, because chaos takes little control. Moving follows closely and marks the start of conscious control. Bind or protect is a focused form of reshaping and the first one people reach for when thinking about safety. Reshaping follows naturally, and creating is the ultimate.

| Domain | Sense | Destroy | Move | Bind or protect | Reshape | Create |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reality | Detect what is altered or false | Erase facts, unmake a rule | Shift which outcome occurs | Lock a fact against change | Rewrite local rules | Make new facts and rules |
| Time | Precognition, retrocognition | Erase moments | Time travel, skipping ahead | Time stop, temporal shield | Slow, speed up, loop | Insert moments, branch a timeline |
| Space | Spatial awareness, mapping | Collapse or close space | Teleportation, portals, flight | Barriers, sealed pockets | Fold, stretch, scale space | Open dimensions, pocket spaces |
| Matter | Sense structure and composition | Shatter, disassemble | Telekinesis | Hardening, force shells | Density, shape, transmutation | Construct, conjure mass |
| Mind | Telepathy, emotion reading | Erase thoughts, break will | Move attention or memory | Mental shields | Illusion, memory editing | Plant thoughts |
| Energy | Sense fields and radiation | Drain, dissipate | Redirect and carry energy | Absorb or shield | Convert between forms | Generate energy, hard light |
| Life | Sense health and vitality | Harm, wither | Move life force between bodies | Regeneration, immunity | Shapeshifting, growth | Heal, revive, grow new tissue |

The Reality row and the Time bind cell are the thinnest, since few characters show those verbs cleanly. Treat them as candidates to define rather than established powers.


## Mastery

Mastery in a domain has two parts: technique, which is the six verbs, and drawing from the domain's source, the wellspring its power comes from. Each domain has its own source, and a student has to learn to find it and pull from it. A beginner can know a technique and barely draw any power. A master has both and sees the domain from a new angle. It works like learning an instrument or a martial art, where chaos comes quickly and control takes years.

A student moves through five levels. The percentage is the share of a domain's total mastery time spent at that level before advancing.

| Level | Name | Technique | Drawing from the source | Share of mastery time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Beginner | Order | Sensing and destroying, plus weak movement. Sloppy, but learning to make sense of chaos and manage the intensity of the power. | Barely draws from the source. Some get lucky and have small bursts of power. | 15% |
| 2. Intermediate | Functional | Bind or protect, stronger movement, and weak, sloppy creating. The work now is getting stronger and cleaner. | Controls the pull, but inefficiently and exhaustingly. More responsible with how power is used, but tires quickly because they have not learned to manage the draw or refine its precision. | 40% |
| 3. Advanced | Well rounded and dangerous | Strong movement. Destruction happens only on purpose. Creating works through a few predictable go-tos used with confidence. Builds counterattacks and chaining sequences, and rarely errs unless something trips them. | Has the draw figured out, but it falters in battle, in competition against another magician, and in tricky situations. Skilled, not yet clever. | 25% |
| 4. Expert | Devil in the details | Nearly a master, missing only master-level sensing, creating, and reshaping. Learning those also allows detailed destruction. Starts to see connections and intuit mastery in other domains. | No problems drawing. The only limit left is skill. | 20% |
| 5. Master | Beginning again | Master of one domain and Intermediate in the others. The journey starts over with a new perspective. | Drawing is no longer a limit, and the source is understood well enough to start learning again from a new perspective. | Not applicable |

Intermediate is the longest level because it is where sloppy power becomes clean power, and that change is slow. The drawing column is a proposal built from the technique levels and can be adjusted.


## Years to mastery

Mastering a domain takes 60 years times its dominance: 60 years for Reality, 52.2 for Time, and 28.2 for Life. That total is split 15%, 40%, 25%, and 20% across the four steps from Beginner to Master. The total covers both technique and learning to draw from the source.

Years spent at each level before advancing:

| Domain | Dominance | Beginner to Intermediate | Intermediate to Advanced | Advanced to Expert | Expert to Master | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reality | 100% | 9.0 | 24.0 | 15.0 | 12.0 | 60.0 |
| Time | 87% | 7.8 | 20.9 | 13.1 | 10.4 | 52.2 |
| Space | 70% | 6.3 | 16.8 | 10.5 | 8.4 | 42.0 |
| Matter | 57% | 5.1 | 13.7 | 8.6 | 6.8 | 34.2 |
| Mind | 53% | 4.8 | 12.7 | 8.0 | 6.4 | 31.8 |
| Energy | 50% | 4.5 | 12.0 | 7.5 | 6.0 | 30.0 |
| Life | 47% | 4.2 | 11.3 | 7.1 | 5.6 | 28.2 |

Total years of study at which each level is reached:

| Domain | Intermediate | Advanced | Expert | Master |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reality | 9.0 | 33.0 | 48.0 | 60.0 |
| Time | 7.8 | 28.7 | 41.8 | 52.2 |
| Space | 6.3 | 23.1 | 33.6 | 42.0 |
| Matter | 5.1 | 18.8 | 27.4 | 34.2 |
| Mind | 4.8 | 17.5 | 25.4 | 31.8 |
| Energy | 4.5 | 16.5 | 24.0 | 30.0 |
| Life | 4.2 | 15.5 | 22.6 | 28.2 |

Values are rounded to 0.1 year, so a row can differ from its total by 0.1.

A student who starts at 18 would master Reality at 78 and Time at 70.2, and would reach only Advanced at about 51.0 and 46.7. Life, Energy, and Mind can be mastered by about 50. The scale of Reality and Time is the case for treating them as restricted.


## How a school could be structured

An eight-year school for students 18 and older fits the numbers for the lighter domains, but the heavier ones need more time. Four years of shared base teach every student the beginner verbs, and how to draw from the source, across the open domains, and four years of focus take them into one domain.

| Years | Phase | What is taught |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Base: sensing | Sensing in all seven domains, including finding each domain's source. Reality and Time are observation only. Discipline and safety rules. |
| 2 | Base: destroying | Supervised destruction in the five open domains, and the first lessons in drawing from each source. Students learn to measure and manage how much power they pull. |
| 3 | Base: moving | Weak movement in each open domain, steadier drawing, and the first cross-domain exercises. |
| 4 | Base: bind or protect | Shields and safety measures, how domains interact, and a containment seminar on Reality and Time. The wardens select students for Reality or Time. Everyone else declares a focus. |
| 5 | Focus | Stronger movement and sturdier bind or protect in the chosen domain, with a steadier draw. Selected students begin Reality or Time under the wardens. |
| 6 | Focus | First creating, sloppy by design, and the basics of chaining. |
| 7 | Focus | An introduction to reshaping and to counters. |
| 8 | Focus: capstone | A few reliable go-to creations, a supervised practical, and a plan for self-directed study toward mastery. |

**Open and restricted domains.** Space, Matter, Energy, Mind, and Life are open as a focus. Reality and Time are restricted, and the wardens of the school choose who studies them. Every student learns to sense the source of all seven domains and to recognize and contain Reality and Time use. Learning to draw from the source of Reality or Time is reserved for students the wardens select, by invitation, at the end of year 4. Each selected student takes one of the two as a focus and may be trained as a warden afterward. Even a full-time student needs 9.0 years in Reality and 7.8 in Time just to reach Intermediate, more than the 4.8 years the program gives them, so selected students continue under the wardens after graduation. A student who starts at 18 reaches Advanced at about 51.0 in Reality and 46.7 in Time, so a warden's seniority is measured in decades.

**What eight years produces.** The model gives each student the four focus years plus a fifth of the base, 0.8 year, in their domain: 4.8 years in total. For selected students this is an upper bound, since the base years teach them only to sense Reality and Time.

| Focus | Years to reach Intermediate | Years in domain at graduation | Share of mastery time | Level at graduation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Life | 4.2 | 4.8 | 17% | Intermediate, with 0.6 year to spare |
| Energy | 4.5 | 4.8 | 16% | Intermediate, with 0.3 year to spare |
| Mind | 4.8 | 4.8 | 15% | Intermediate, just barely |
| Matter | 5.1 | 4.8 | 14% | Late Beginner, 0.3 year short |
| Space | 6.3 | 4.8 | 11% | Beginner, 1.5 years short |
| Time (selected) | 7.8 | Up to 4.8 | 9% | Beginner, at least 3.0 years short, continues under the wardens |
| Reality (selected) | 9.0 | Up to 4.8 | 8% | Beginner, at least 4.2 years short, continues under the wardens |

A fixed 4+4 does not give every student the same result, because mastery times differ by a factor of one and a half across the open domains. Two adjustments keep the structure and close the gap: a residency after graduation of a few months for Matter and one to two years for Space, and a second minor for Life students, who finish with the most time to spare. Graduates then leave able to pursue mastery on their own, which is what the school is for.


## Boundary cases and open questions

- **Gravity** sits between Space and Matter because mass bends space. This framework files it under Space, since its ceiling is spacetime curvature.
- **Super speed** starts in Space as motion, but its ceiling, your own clock stopping, reaches Time. It is the one power that bridges two domains.
- **Precognition versus Probability.** Precognition perceives how events unfold (Time). Probability changes which outcome occurs (Reality).
- **Souls and resurrection.** Life covers bodies and their processes. Whether raising the dead or controlling a soul needs its own eighth domain is open.
- **Colors.** Reality is white because it contains every other color. If white reads poorly on a page, gold is the fallback.

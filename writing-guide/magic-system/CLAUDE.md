# CLAUDE.md — writing-guide/magic-system/

Two **guidelines** imported from the author's own worldbuilding notes. They are tools for thinking about magic, not facts about this world.

- `seven-domains.md` — what magic can do: seven ranked domains (Reality, Time, Space, Matter, Mind, Energy, Life), the six verbs (sense, destroy, move, bind or protect, reshape, create), the five mastery levels, and years to mastery.
- `magic-management.md` — why magic needs limits, and how a world moves from arrival to stability: the three categories of restriction, the fixed rules, the turning points, and the five stages.

## How to use them
1. Never cite either file as canon. "The Seven Domains says Time is restricted" is a statement about the guideline, not about this world.
2. To decide something for this world, fill in the forms in `../../story-bible/magic/` (the world form first, then one creature form per kind), write the reasoning beside each choice, and propose it to the author. It becomes canon only when he confirms it.
3. If a choice from a guideline would force or contradict a plot point in `story-bible/`, flag the tension rather than resolving it in either direction.

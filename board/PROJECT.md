# Project charter

_Drafted by the lead editor at migration, 2026-10-17. Sections marked TBD need the author at Gate G1 (ticket T-013)._

## What this is
*Romantic Fantasy Anthologies*: an adult romantic-fantasy saga in seven eras and an epilogue, set on prehistoric Earth, where **women lead and men support**, with the gazes deliberately crossed. Built on the same process and prose standards as *Vector Anthologies*.

## Where it stands (2026-10-17)
- A detailed world, migrated from the plan built with the author into `story-bible/`, **not yet audited** (T-001).
- Writing guide carried over from Vector, adapted. The **narrator is open** (T-003) and the **tone ratio provisional** (T-005).
- Magic guidelines imported (Seven Domains, Magic Management); this world's magic is **not yet decided** (T-006, T-007).
- No stories yet. The site is ready to deploy once Pages and the board passphrase are set (T-012).

## How the work divides
Carried over from Vector, to confirm at kickoff: the author decides what happens and writes the outline; `drafting-author` writes the prose, with `women-characters` and `men-characters` on scenes of desire; the author approves.

## Goals
TBD at G1. Working assumption: a repeatable path from an author outline to a drafted, checked, approved story on the site, starting with Era 3 (told first).

## Milestone 1 (proposed)
1. **T-001**, the canon audit.
2. **T-003**, narrator research, and **T-002 / T-004**, market research, in parallel.
3. **T-013**, the kickoff interview, on the audit's findings.
4. Pilot one Era 3 story end to end.

## Constraints
- **The age rule** (`writing-guide/story-laws.md` → 1) is absolute.
- Women lead; men support. The gazes are crossed.
- `story-bible/` is canon. Agents propose; the author decides.
- The magic guidelines are guidelines. Decisions are rationalized and confirmed.
- New agents cost money and need the author's approval first.
- The repo and the reading site are public.
